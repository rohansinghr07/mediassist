Questions

Good to know

Do I need Telegram to visit the clinic?

No. You can walk in and our reception will book a free slot for you. Telegram just lets you book from home and get reminders.

Can I book with a voice message?

Yes. Send a voice note in Hindi or English. The assistant repeats what it heard and asks you to confirm before going ahead.

How do I reschedule or cancel?

On Telegram, open the menu → My Appointments, pick the booking and choose reschedule or cancel. You can also ask at reception.

How far ahead can I book?

Up to 7 days ahead, and at least 30 minutes before the visit time.

When should I arrive?

Please come 10 minutes early and bring old prescriptions and reports, if you have any.

Is a doctor available on Sunday?

No, the clinic is closed on Sunday. In an emergency call 108 or go to the nearest hospital emergency.
