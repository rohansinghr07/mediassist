## Appointments
- Each appointment is 10 minutes.
- Book on this Telegram bot: menu → Book Appointment. You can describe your problem (or send a voice note) and the bot suggests the right department.
- You can book up to 7 days ahead, and at least 30 minutes before the visit time.
- Reschedule or cancel any time from the bot: menu → My Appointments.
- You get reminders 24 hours and 2 hours before your visit.
- Please arrive 10 minutes before your appointment time and bring old prescriptions and reports, if any.
